Register and match your face

The camera automatically captures five poses when it recognizes them. Follow each instruction and hold still briefly.

  • Keep one face centered in the guide; look straight, then to the left and right when asked.
  • Move closer, then slightly farther away.
  • Keep your entire face visible: no cap, mask or hand in front.
  • Use even lighting, with no strong light behind you. Each pose is checked before moving on.
